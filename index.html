YĀN — ask the cards anything

YĀN (ญาณ) is a Thai-first oracle-card app for Android. Pick a deck, ask your question, shuffle and turn the cards. Read them yourself for free every day, or let the YĀN AI read them for you.

Get it on Google Play

  • Read it yourself, free daily — spreads, rich card meanings, a listen button
  • AI reading — ask in your own words; credits per question: 5 (big spreads 10–15)
  • Daily card and monthly identity card, 14 original oracle decks, Thai / English / Chinese

Prices: free sign-up gives 10 credits a day for 15 days. Credit packs 30 / 65 / 100 for ฿29 / ฿59 / ฿79. Monthly, 3-month and annual plans ฿89 / ฿259 / ฿899 with 1,500 credits a month plus 15 free a day. All payments through Google Play.
